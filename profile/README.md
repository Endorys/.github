# Endorys

## Ultrasound-led multiphysics tissue research

Founded by **Sadhu Shobab Bollipo**, Endorys brings together research into conformal sensing, controlled tissue interrogation, response modelling and longitudinal assessment. The programme began with breast applications and expanded to other clinical questions and materials research.

The proposed architecture combines selected acoustic excitation, distributed receivers, geometry/contact calibration, synchronized acquisition and external computation.

### Explore the platform

- **Matrix**: conformal sensing surface
- **Insight**: reconstruction and inference
- **Interface**: coupling, mechanical interrogation and disposable components
- **Therapy**: therapeutic acoustics research
- **Surgical**: surgical and procedural tissue awareness
- **Continuum**: longitudinal data and clinical integration
- **Evidence**: validation and provenance
- **Explore**: clinical and materials opportunity discovery
- **Bench**: physical experiments
- **Challenge**: critique and falsification
- **Vascular**: vascular application research

### Current stage

This is a research concept and documentation baseline. Proposed capabilities require experimental validation. No diagnostic or therapeutic performance is established. Reuse licenses have not yet been selected.

### Participate

We welcome focused research questions, reproducible experiments, evidence reviews and constructive technical critique. Start with the [platform repository](https://github.com/Endorys/platform). Please do not submit confidential information or patient data.
